Welcome to Albany Cub Scouts Pack 3

Are you ready for more s'mores, skits, badges, and outdoor fun?

Albany Cub Scouts are kids in kindergarten through fifth grade who join a pack to go hiking, play games, learn skills, make friends, and much, much more.

Cub Scouting means "doing". You have lots to do as a Cub Scout—hiking, camping, crafts, games, sports, songs, stories, and puzzles, to name just a few things. Much of the fun happens right in the den and pack.

Scouts also participate in events like the annual Blue and Gold banquet, the Pinewood Derby, an Egg Drop, and our Pancake Breakfast. Whatever it is that you enjoy, you'll have a chance to do it in Cub Scouting!

"Albany Pack 3 has a long and proud history of service to Albany's youth and to the community as a whole."

How We're Organized

Pack Organization

Albany Cub Scouts Pack 3 is made up of dens organized by age group. Different grade levels work toward achieving the rank for that year.

Lion

Kindergarten

Our youngest scouts start their adventure with fun activities and family involvement!

Tiger

1st Grade

Building confidence and new skills while exploring the world around them.

Wolf

2nd Grade

Exploring the outdoors and learning about our community together.

Bear

3rd Grade

Taking on bigger challenges and developing teamwork skills.

Webelos

4th Grade

We Be Loyal Scouts - preparing for the next step in their journey.

Arrow of Light

5th Grade

The highest rank in Cub Scouting before bridging to Scouts BSA.

Cub Scout rank badges are trademarks of Scouting America, used with permission.

Pack Meetings

The whole Pack (all dens) meets once a month on Thursdays at the Veterans Memorial Building, usually from 6:30 to 8pm. The entire family (including siblings) is welcome.

Veterans Memorial Hall, 1325 Portland Avenue, Albany, CA

Den Meetings

Each den establishes its own meeting schedule, usually 1-2 times per month. At den meetings, Scouts work on advancements, learn skills, and always have a great time.

For Lion and Tiger Scouts, a parent is expected to join their Scout at meetings.

Pack 3 is in the Herms District of the Golden Gate Council and is chartered by the American Legion Post 292 of Albany

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in Albany Pack 3.

Anyone in kindergarten through fifth grades! Starting in 2018, we welcomed girls to join the cub scouts. We will be establishing dens at the outset of the Scouting season based on the number of Scouts who sign up in each grade. Den sizes are 10 or fewer. Albany's Pack 3 has always prided itself on being an inclusive unit that represents the diversity of our community. We are strongly committed to creating a welcoming environment for all children who wish to join our Pack. We are proud to be designated as an Inclusive Unit by Scouts for Equality and our leaders wear the "Scouts for Equality" badge on their uniforms to signal our dedication to a Scouting movement rooted in equality and free of discrimination.

Cub Scouts is a family program for all kids interested in having fun, learning life skills, building community, exploring the outdoors, becoming leaders and good citizens, and making friends. The basic structure of the group is a "pack" – which includes the whole group of Scouts, from kindergarten through fifth grades. Each grade has one or more "dens" that meet in smaller groups to work on adventures to earn their rank. Cub Scouts complete adventures as they work to achieve their rank for that year. The adventures are different for each rank, allowing Cub Scouts to learn new age-appropriate skills and explore new topics each year. At the end of the year, if a Scout has met all of the requirements for that year, he or she will receive the rank (i.e. a first grader would receive the "Tiger" rank).

• Kindergarten = Lion • First Grade = Tiger • Second Grade = Wolf • Third Grade = Bear • Fourth Grade = Webelos (We Be Loyal Scouts) • Fifth Grade = Arrow of Light (AOL) In their fifth grade year, scouts "bridge" to Boy Scouts. This involves visiting local troops and choosing one that the scout will participate in and contribute to during their further adventures in Scouting.

Pack dues are $120 per year and include food, badges, and activities for your scout all year. This goes directly to the Albany Pack to cover activities for scouts and supplies. You must also register with the National organization which is about $230. This includes things like insurance and a magazine. Lion and Tiger Scouts also require a parent to register with Pack 3 as an adult leader for $15. The basic uniform consists of a shirt, hat, neckerchief, neckerchief slide, and the pack and council patches. This can add up to $100 or so. (Our pack re-uses shirts and gear so check with us before buying!) The hat, neckerchief and slide will change with the different grades, but the same blue shirt can be used from kindergarten through third grade. In fourth and fifth grades, Scouts switch to a tan-colored shirt. We want every family to be able to participate. If your family needs help with registration costs, please speak with our Cubmaster about financial assistance.

The Pack meets once a month on a Thursday evening from 6:30 to 7:30. Dens meet once or twice monthly on a day selected by the den. Throughout the year, families can also participate in campouts, hikes, community service projects, and other Pack traditions. We typically hold at least two family campouts each year—one in the fall and one in the spring—with additional campouts offered when possible. Other favorite activities include: Other activities include: • Pinewood Derby (January) • Pancake Breakfast (February) • Bake sale (April) • Community service projects • Fall and spring hikes Pack 3 is a volunteer-run organization, and parent participation is an important part of making our program possible. We ask each family to contribute at least 16 volunteer hours per year. There are lots of ways to help—from assisting at events and campouts to taking on small behind-the-scenes jobs—and we work with families to find opportunities that fit their schedules and interests. Parents of Lion and Tiger Scouts are also required to complete Safeguarding Youth Training online, which takes approximately one hour.

Registration Always Open

Join Albany Pack 3

Registration is ongoing throughout the year — we welcome new and returning families anytime. Follow the steps below to get started.

1

Complete the Online Registration Form

Pack registration is ongoing and we welcome new and returning families throughout the year. Fill out the form to get your family started, and email registration@albanycubscouts.org with any questions about the application process.

Complete Registration Form
2

Register and Pay National BSA Dues

Register (or re-register) with the council and national BSA organization. You will be asked to pay the national fees. The National BSA fee is $230 per scout.

Register with BSA
3

Pay Pack Dues

Pack dues are $120 per year and cover food, badges, and activities for your scout all year. Please pay via:

  • Zelle to treasurer@albanycubscouts.org
  • Paper check made payable to "Albany Pack 3 Cub Scouts"

Please clearly indicate the name of the scout you are paying for in the memo/details field. If your family needs help with registration costs, please speak with our Cubmaster about financial assistance.

4

Purchase a Uniform

The basic uniform consists of a shirt, hat, neckerchief, neckerchief slide, and the pack and council patches — this can add up to $100 or so. Albany Pack 3 works hard to re-use old uniforms, so check with us before buying! We plan to do a bulk uniform buy each year; more information will be sent out.

You can also order online or visit a Scout store (in Pleasant Hill or San Rafael). Be sure to tell them you are with Albany Pack 003, part of the Golden Gate District, and what rank your scout is. The same blue shirt works for Kindergarten through 3rd grade; 4th and 5th graders wear a tan shirt. While there, pick up the handbook for your scout's grade. Shop online at scoutstuff.org, and see the BSA Insignia Guide for patch placement.

5

(Adults) Complete Youth Protection Training (YPT)

Youth Protection Training is highly recommended for all Pack 3 adults and REQUIRED for all parents/guardians of Kindergarten (Lions) and 1st graders (Tigers), and for any adult volunteering to help lead activities with kids.

NOTE: Everyone who is required to take YPT must do it EVERY year, even if your certificate says it is good for two years. Please send your completed certificate by OCTOBER 15 to registration@albanycubscouts.org.

Start Youth Protection Training
6

(Adults) Livescan and Mandatory Reporter Training

The Cubmaster will communicate with you if this is required. Please email registration@albanycubscouts.org when you have completed this requirement.
